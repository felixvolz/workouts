What comes from the book?

The foundation is Original BBB with 5’s PRO: main work uses 65/75/85%, 70/80/90%, then 75/85/95% of training max, five reps each. Supplemental work is at a constant 50% of training max.

The optional three-set setup is an adaptation. We combined the three-set idea described under Forever BBB Light with Original BBB’s constant percentage. It is not one exact printed template. Full BBB uses five sets of ten. Changing the selector changes volume only.

BBB assistance targets are 25–50 push reps, 25–50 pull reps and 0–50 single-leg/core reps per session. For BBB, Wendler favours abdominal work and advises against extra lower-back work and much single-leg work. The specific exercises, set counts and schedule here are our practical choices.

The book advises against BBB for true beginners; its dedicated beginner template is Beginner Prep School. This page tracks the BBB approach you chose.

Bench numbers assume actual bench press, with your estimate updated to 60 kg from the week of 26 October 2026. If you are floor pressing instead, establish a separate estimate and enter it before using these loads. A heavier bar than the target is flagged rather than silently prescribed.

Next-cycle training-max increases are optional and require your review. The cards project cycle increases but label them as unconfirmed until reviewed. The app does not prescribe a deload. This calendar repeats three-week lifting blocks for planning purposes. It does not schedule recovery automatically: after two cycles, review the book’s recovery/7th Week Protocol and adjust your training before continuing.
